Descriptions reflect current positioning and public sites reviewed August 2026.

## 01  Do Udyog — doudyog.com

A digital operating centre for MSMEs and growing businesses.

**Purpose.** Helps entrepreneurs, shops, service firms and small industries establish a trusted business identity and access structured support for compliance, operations and growth.

**Built.** Business profiles and directory; registration and verification; compliance and growth sections; service discovery; partner access; business dashboard.

**Potential.** Primary digital entry point through which small businesses access jobs, services, customers and commerce across Do Galaxy.

## 02  Do Vishram — dovishram.com

A trusted discovery platform for stays and curated rest experiences.

**Purpose.** Travellers, families and professionals seeking hotels, homestays, resorts, short stays and packaged rest experiences.

**Built.** Stay discovery; accommodation categories; curated rest packages; enquiry and support; trust promise; partner onboarding.

**Potential.** Regional network of verified stays, short breaks, wellness-led travel and local hospitality.

## 03  Do Rojgar — dorojgar.com

A hyperlocal employment marketplace.

**Purpose.** Local jobs, skilled work, office roles and gigs for seekers; a simple channel for employers to find talent.

**Built.** Job search and listings; candidate profiles; resume builder; application tracking; employer posting; candidate and employer dashboards.

**Potential.** Bridge local skills and local demand — SMEs, service businesses, frontline and regional markets.

## 04  Do Swagat — doswagat.com

A coordinated marketplace for celebrations, events and hospitality.

**Purpose.** Families and organisations plan weddings, parties and corporate events with venues and trusted partners in one flow.

**Built.** Venue and service catalogues; caterer, decorator, photographer profiles; curated packages; enquiry; request tracking; partner pages.

**Potential.** Organise a fragmented local event-services market into planning, booking and partner management.

## 05  Do Rishta — dorishta.com

A trusted, family-friendly matrimonial platform.

**Purpose.** Individuals and families discover compatible partners through trust, verification and relevance.

**Built.** Profile registration; verified-profile positioning; match discovery; compatibility matching; catalogue; membership; support pages.

**Potential.** Differentiate on trust, regional and community depth, family participation, guided discovery.

## 06  Do Bajar — dobajar.com

A digital marketplace connecting sellers, products and everyday buyers.

**Purpose.** Businesses and local sellers get digital visibility; customers discover products in a structured marketplace.

**Built.** Marketplace identity and domain; seller-to-customer positioning; storefront-led discovery; selling channel for Do Udyog businesses.

**Potential.** Commerce layer of Do Galaxy — from digital identity to discovery, reach and transactions.

## The larger opportunity

- **Independent value** — each product grows in its own sector and partnership network.
- **Shared trust** — verification, profiles and partner quality as a common layer.
- **Cross-product journeys** — a Do Udyog business hires on Do Rojgar, runs an event on Do Swagat, sells on Do Bajar.
- **Scalable expansion** — product by product, region by region.

**Vision.** Do Galaxy aims to bring trusted, practical and locally relevant digital services into one coherent universe.
